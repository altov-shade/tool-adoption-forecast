All data here is fictional. The company, headcount and usage numbers are invented for this demo.

The probability depends on the assumptions. It is the share of 4,000 fixed-seed simulated futures in which week-four active users reach 120. Each person moves between never tried, inactive and active: 30% of never-tried people start each week (first-time activation), 18% of inactive people return (reactivation), and the retention rate changes only between the two scenarios. Assumptions are illustrative, not fitted to real data; different assumptions would give a different chance.
